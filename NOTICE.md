# Third-party brand assets

The PWA icon graphics are raster derivatives of the DeepSeek fish logo from the
[DeepSeek Harness favicon](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/apps/web/public/favicon.svg)
(official [DeepSeek Harness repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness), MIT license).
The logo has been recolored white on a solid `#4D6BFE` background with safe
margins and exported at 180, 192 and 512 pixels.

respective owners. This independently maintained plugin is not endorsed by
